# Traceability matrix

Requirement to specification to decision to ROADMAP task. Generated by the script `traceability.py` of the workspace root (next to `ROADMAP.md`) from the documents themselves: a requirement is linked to every SDD that names its identifier, to every ROADMAP task whose text or `Refs` name it, and to the ADRs that name it plus the (at most four) ADRs cited most often in the `Refs` of those tasks. `ROADMAP.md` lives in the workspace root, outside this repository (D-025). Regenerate after changing a PRD, an SDD or the ROADMAP; do not edit by hand.

How to read it: an empty SDD cell or an empty task cell is a gap and is listed again at the end. Phase letters: `P` Prototype, `A` ALPHA, `B` BETA. Non-functional requirements have no phase of their own: each is verified by the tasks listed.
