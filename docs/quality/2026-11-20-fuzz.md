## Findings

- `FuzzRetryAfter` found that `retryAfter("9227000000")` of the webhook destination overflowed a `time.Duration` into a negative wait. Fixed in `service/publish/destinations/webhook.go` (the cap is applied to the number of seconds); the input stays as the regression case `testdata/fuzz/FuzzRetryAfter/2f261fc7602f5d07`, and the threat is T-100 of SDD-007 with `TestSecurity_T100_ReceiverRetryAfterNeverMakesAnOverflowedOrLongWait`.
- `FuzzTokenResponse`, added in the review of the epic for the token answer of an OAuth sign-in, found the same overflow in `expires_in`: 18446744074 seconds became a lifetime of 290 ms, so every use of the connection would refresh its token, and 9227000000 or a negative number became a negative lifetime. Fixed in `service/connections/oauth/oauth.go` (`lifetime`: a value that is not positive or does not fit a duration is no lifetime, and the caller takes its default of one hour), with `TestTokenLifetimeThatOverflowsIsUnknown`; SDD-020 section 6 states the rule.
- `FuzzFontHeader` failed once for load, not for the input: the input it wrote passes in 1 s alone. `scripts/fuzz.sh` now runs 4 workers by default (`FUZZ_PARALLEL`), and the failing input was not kept.

New targets of this epic: `core/image` `FuzzApplyArgs` and `FuzzDecode`, `core/subtitle` `FuzzParse`, `core/library` `FuzzParseSkill`, `service/connections/oauth` `FuzzParseEnvelope` and `FuzzTokenResponse`, `service/publish/destinations` `FuzzRetryAfter`, `web/app/http` `FuzzSignInRedirect`. The command core of the video document (`FuzzApplyBatch`, `FuzzApplyArgs`) and the parsers of earlier epics ran with them.
