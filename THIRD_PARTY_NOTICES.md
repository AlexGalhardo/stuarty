## Base image packages (Debian)

The container image is built on a Debian slim base. Its operating system packages are listed in `/usr/share/doc/vukem-video/base-image-packages.txt` inside the image, and the licence of each is the `copyright` file Debian keeps in `/usr/share/doc/<package>/`. They are an operating system, not part of the product: the product bundles and links no GPL component.
