# Write a hook

The first line decides whether anyone sees the rest. On a short video you have about three seconds: the line must say, in words and on the screen, why this video is for this viewer. This skill is adapted from the social content skill of the marketing skills set named above (MIT licence); the text and the steps are rewritten for Stuarty.

## Steps

1. Read the brief: `brief:show --project <project id>`. Take one specific fact from it: a number, a result, a mistake, a person, a before and an after. A hook with no fact is a slogan.
2. Run `recall-learnings` for hooks that worked or were cut before.
3. Write three hooks, each from a different family, and keep each under twelve words:
   - Curiosity: "I was wrong about [common belief]." "[Result] in [short time], and the cause is not what you think."
   - Value: "How to [outcome] without [usual pain]." "[Number] things that [outcome]."
   - Story: "Last week [unexpected thing] happened." "Three months ago I [bad state]. Today [good state]."
   - Contrarian: "Stop [common practice]. It cost me [specific result]." "Most [audience] get [topic] wrong."
   - Contrast: "[Option A] or [option B]? Only one of them [result]." Both options must be real, and the video must pick one.
4. Fill each one with the fact from step 1. Check each against the list below, and rewrite what fails.
5. Show the person the three, say which you would use and why in one line, and let them choose. Keep the chosen line in the script (`short-script`).

## No machine-sounding phrases

Viewers scroll past text that reads as generated. Never write:

- A contrast reveal: "It is not X, it is Y." Say Y, with the reason.
- A list of negations: "No X, no Y, no Z." Say what does happen.
- A question answered at once: "The result? Three times faster."
- Stock phrases: "Say goodbye to", "Unlock the power of", "Take it to the next level", "Here is the thing".
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Let that sink in".
- Made-up drama: "I was terrified" with no real story behind it.

## Rules

- A hook promises only what the video delivers. A promise the video breaks costs more than a plain hook.
- Use the words of the audience in the brief, not the words of the product.
- Do not invent a number, a result or a name that the brief and the person did not give.
- Write in the language of the video.
