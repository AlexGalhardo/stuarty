## You will learn

- what `E_NO_ENCODER` says and what was tried;
- how to read the encoder check of the doctor;
- three ways out: the bundled FFmpeg, a hardware encoder and your own libx264 build.

## The idea

An export needs an H.264 encoder, and the product only trusts one that passed a real test encode on your machine. It tries, in order, `h264_videotoolbox`, `h264_nvenc`, `h264_qsv`, `h264_amf`, `h264_mf`, `h264_vaapi` and `libopenh264`; `libx264` joins only when the FFmpeg is your own and `VUKEM_VIDEO_ALLOW_USER_X264` is `true`. If none passes, the export fails with `E_NO_ENCODER`; the message lists each candidate and why it failed (not in this FFmpeg build, or the first line of its error). The detection is cached for 24 hours.

## Quick start

1. Run `vukem-video doctor --full`. The `ffmpeg.encoder` check prints the chosen encoder and each candidate with its status and time, or `E_NO_ENCODER`.
2. Pick the way out that fits:
   - use the FFmpeg that ships with Vukem Video (the container image and the installers), which includes OpenH264;
   - install the driver of your GPU, so that a hardware encoder passes the test;
   - point `VUKEM_VIDEO_FFMPEG` at your own build that has `libx264` and set `VUKEM_VIDEO_ALLOW_USER_X264=true`. The licence consequences are yours: do not redistribute it.
3. Run the doctor again, then export.

```bash
vukem-video doctor --full
VUKEM_VIDEO_ALLOW_USER_X264=true vukem-video serve
```

To test one encoder only, set `VUKEM_VIDEO_ENCODER` to its FFmpeg name. Then only that one is tried and the product never swaps it silently.

## Common mistakes

- Forcing an encoder the machine does not have (`VUKEM_VIDEO_ENCODER=h264_nvenc` without an NVIDIA driver).
- Setting `VUKEM_VIDEO_ALLOW_USER_X264=true` while the FFmpeg is the bundled one: `libx264` is used only with your own FFmpeg.
- Running in a container or a virtual machine without the device nodes that a hardware encoder needs. The OpenH264 encoder of the bundled FFmpeg needs none.
- Reading `E_FFMPEG_MISSING` as the same thing. See [FFmpeg is not found](/docs/troubleshooting/ffmpeg-missing).
