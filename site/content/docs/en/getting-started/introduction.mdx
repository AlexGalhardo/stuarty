Stuarty is a place to create, edit, manage and publish your content. It is open source (MIT), you run it on your own computer or your own server, and it is built so that an AI agent can do anything a person does in it: everything in the interface is also in the command line, the HTTP API and MCP.

## What it does

- **Video.** Import clips, cut and arrange them on a timeline, add captions, animate, and export a checked MP4. The first complete flow is a short vertical video with captions and a thumbnail, from the idea to the published post: [follow it step by step](/docs/guide/first-short-video).
- **Images.** An image editor with layers, templates, adjustments, filters and masks, for thumbnails and covers.
- **Writing.** Scripts, captions and posts, kept with their history.
- **Projects.** Every production is a project with its assets, its brief and its publications.
- **AI, on your terms.** You bring your own access: an API key, Sign in with ChatGPT, OpenRouter or your local agent. You choose what is sent and what it may cost.
- **Publishing.** Your own developer apps for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok. Nothing is published until you approve it.

## What stays on your machine

Your files, your projects, your tokens and your credentials. A credential lives in the vault of the workspace and no tool, page or agent can read it back. With no connection configured, Stuarty opens no outbound connection at all, and it has no telemetry. [What leaves your machine, and when](/docs/what-leaves-your-machine) lists every case.

## How it fits together

An agent edits through **MCP**, the **CLI** or the **HTTP API**. A person edits the same project in a **browser** or in the **desktop application**, which is a window over the same interface. All of them go through one command core written in Go, so a person and an agent see the same result and the same history. The interface never computes the result of an edit: it draws what the core says and sends commands.
