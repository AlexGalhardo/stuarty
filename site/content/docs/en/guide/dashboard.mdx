## You will learn

- what each group of the sidebar holds;
- what the agent bar at the top says and what its buttons do;
- that the browser and the desktop application show the same pages.

## The idea

When you open Stuarty you land on the **dashboard**: one home with a sidebar that leads to each part of the work. The desktop application is a window over the same pages, so what you learn in one is true in the other. The sidebar collapses to icons, and on a phone it opens as a drawer.

The home greets you by the part of the day and the name of your account. It says how many productions are in progress and how many requests wait for your yes. It shows the project in progress with its path and one button for its current step, and it lists **Your studio** (the areas of the work) and **Waiting for you** (the requests that wait, with the way to the queue). With no project yet, it shows three steps: say what you want to create, fill in the briefing, follow the path to publication.

## The agent bar

A bar under the top of every page tells you what the agent is doing, so that nothing happens out of sight. From left to right it shows:

- **Agent**: the state, one of waiting for you, working, asks your permission, you denied it, finished, or failed, and the project it works on;
- **Skill**: whether a skill is loaded in this session, available or none;
- **Tool**: the last tool the agent called and its status, or "No tool called yet";
- **Next**: the next step the agent reported, or "No next step reported";
- **Open chat**, or **Connect an agent** when none is connected.

When the agent asks your permission, the bar shows the request with **Allow once** and **Deny**. What and where are as the agent described the call; Stuarty cannot check them. Only a person can answer, in the desktop app or signed in with a password. See [The agent chat](/docs/guide/agent-chat).

The **bell** at the top lists everything that waits for you, from the chat and from the Approvals queue: what will happen, to what, where, at what cost and whether it can be undone. **Review in Approvals** opens the queue.

## Quick start

1. Open the dashboard and choose **Projects**, then **New project**.
2. Pick **Video editor** or **Image editor** from the sidebar to work on it.
3. Open **Connections** when you want a provider or a network: nothing is connected until you do it.

Everything a section does is also a command: `stuarty project:list`, `stuarty asset:list`, `stuarty publication:list`, `stuarty memory:list` and so on. An agent uses the same commands.
