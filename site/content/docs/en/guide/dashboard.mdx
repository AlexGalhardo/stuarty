## You will learn

- what each section of the sidebar holds;
- that the browser and the desktop application show the same pages.

## The idea

When you open Stuarty you land on the **dashboard**: one home with a sidebar that leads to each part of the work. The desktop application is a window over the same pages, so what you learn in one is true in the other. The sidebar collapses to icons, and on a phone it opens as a drawer.

| Section | What it holds | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Projects** | every production, with its brief, texts, timelines and publications | [Projects](/docs/projects) |
| **Edit Video** | the video editor: timeline, captions, effects and export | [Basic timeline editing](/docs/guide/timeline) |
| **Edit Image** | the image editor: layers, text and thumbnails | [The image editor](/docs/guide/image-editor) |
| **Write** | scripts, copy and captions, with their revisions | [Writing](/docs/guide/writing) |
| **Visual Assets**, **Video Assets**, **Sound Assets** | the library of every image, clip, sound and colour table you have | [The asset library](/docs/guide/asset-library) |
| **Publish** | the calendar, and the publications of your networks | [The publishing calendar](/docs/guide/publishing-calendar) |
| **Skills**, **Prompts**, **Memory** | the way you work, as files you own | [Memory, skills and prompts](/docs/guide/memory-skills-prompts) |
| **Connections** | the destinations you connected: AI providers, networks, sign-ins | [AI providers](/docs/guide/ai-providers) |
| **Settings** | language, theme, shortcuts, the desktop options | [Settings](/docs/guide/settings) |

Approvals live where the work does: a request appears on the page of the thing that made it, and the queue is on its own page ([Approvals](/docs/guide/approvals)).

## Quick start

1. Open the dashboard and choose **Projects**, then **New project**.
2. Pick **Edit Video** or **Edit Image** from the sidebar to work on it.
3. Open **Connections** when you want a provider or a network: nothing is connected until you do it.

Everything a section does is also a command: `stuarty project:list`, `stuarty asset:list`, `stuarty publication:list`, `stuarty memory:list` and so on. An agent uses the same commands.
