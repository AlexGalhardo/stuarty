## You will learn

- how to correct the colour of a clip;
- how to give text an entrance and an exit with a preset;
- how to apply a colour filter and a blur to a clip;
- what the product does not do.

## The idea

Colour correction is three numbers on a clip: `brightness` (-1 to 1, default 0), `contrast` (0 to 2, default 1) and `saturation` (0 to 2, default 1). A value equal to the default clears the stored one, so a clip that is back at the defaults has no correction at all. Both the preview and the export apply the correction through the same [render plan](/docs/concepts/render).

For text, captions, clips and shapes there are six **animation presets**: `fade_up`, `fade_down`, `pop`, `zoom_in`, `slide_left` and `slide_right`. A preset writes keyframes (or, for the slides, a transition) on the `in` or the `out` edge, 0.4 seconds long unless you give a duration. The inspector has an Animation section with the preset and the edge.

Text styling (font, weight, size, colour, stroke, shadow and background) is described in [Text and captions](/docs/guide/text-and-captions). Fades and slides between items are in [Transitions](/docs/guide/transitions).

### Filters and blur

`clip.set_filter` sets one filter of a fixed list, `grayscale`, `sepia`, `invert`, `warm` or `cool` (`none` is the default), and `amount`, from 0 (the original) to 1 (the full filter). `clip.set_blur` sets a Gaussian blur radius in pixels at the project resolution, from 0 to 100. Both are constant over the clip: they cannot be keyframed. A filter set to `none` with amount 1, or a blur of 0, clears the stored value. The filter is applied after the colour correction and before the blur, in the preview and in the export alike.

```bash
vukem-video edit --project prj_7k2m9x4q1b clip.set_filter item_id=itm_m1n2p3q4r5   filter=sepia amount:=0.6
vukem-video edit --project prj_7k2m9x4q1b clip.set_blur item_id=itm_m1n2p3q4r5 radius:=12
```

An MCP agent sends the same operations through `edit`. The colour correction, the filter and the blur have no inspector section yet: use the command palette (`Ctrl+K`, `Cmd+K` on macOS), the CLI or an agent. There are no LUTs.

A preset replaces the keyframes of the same properties inside its edge and keeps the rest of the animation.

## Common mistakes

- Setting a contrast above 2 or a brightness outside -1 to 1: `E_VALIDATION`.
- Applying a preset, a filter or a blur to an audio item: `E_TYPE_MISMATCH`.
- A blur radius above 100 or a filter amount outside 0 to 1: `E_VALIDATION`.
- A preset duration longer than the item: the command is refused.
