## You will learn

- what the <Term id="safe-area">safe area</Term> is and how big it is for each format;
- how to show it over the preview;
- how the lint rule finds text outside it.

## The idea

Social apps cover the edges of a vertical video with their own buttons, names and captions. The **safe area** is the rectangle where text stays readable. The core computes it with one function, so the guide in the editor and the lint rule always agree.

| Frame | Left and right | Top | Bottom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical (width divided by height at most 0.6, such as 9:16) | 6% of the width | 14% of the height | 35% of the height |
| Any other size | 5% | 5% | 5% |

The vertical margins follow the Meta Reels guidance; no other platform's margins are verified, so the other sizes use 5% on every side.

## Quick start

Press **Safe area** in the preview toolbar. The editor draws the rectangle as a dashed outline and dims the band outside it. The numbers come from the core; the editor does not compute them.

An agent finds the same problem with the lint:

<Tabs>
<Tab title="CLI">

```bash
vukem-video lint --project prj_7k2m9x4q1b
```

</Tab>
<Tab title="MCP">

`lint` with `{"project_id": "prj_7k2m9x4q1b"}`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

`L_SAFE_AREA` is a warning on a text or caption item whose box (every line of its widest layout, rotation applied) reaches outside the rectangle. Move the item inside, or give the caption the position `middle` with `track.set_caption_style`.

## Common mistakes

- Treating the warning as an error. It never blocks an export; you decide.
- Moving a caption with `item.set_transform` when the track style positions it. Change the track style instead.
