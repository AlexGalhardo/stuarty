Publications and the approval queue that gates them and costly generations.

## How to reach it

MCP tools: `command_help`. Tools that take `domain=publish`: `command_help`.

CLI: `stuarty publish` lists the commands of the domain and `stuarty publish <verb>` runs one; the full names stay valid.

- `approval:list`: list the approval queue: what waits for a person (pending by default)
- `approval:show`: show one approval request: what will be sent, where, the cost and its state
- `approval:rule:list`: list the rules that approve without asking
- `social:network:list`: list the social networks this build can publish to, with their limits and where they were read
- `social:connect`: start the sign-in to a network with your own developer app; the client secret is read from standard input or a file, never from an argument
- `social:account:list`: list the connected social accounts and whether each is signed in
- `publish:validate`: check a post against the limits of its network and show what a person would approve; sends nothing
- `publish:now`: prepare the publication of an exported video: it waits for a person to approve it in the app, then it is sent; you cannot approve it
- `publish:status`: show the state of a publication: waiting_approval, uploading, processing, published or failed

## Operations

No edit operations yet. The domain is reached through the tools and commands above; operations that a later epic registers in it appear here, and `command_help domain=publish` lists them.
