AI providers and generation: models, prices, text generation, the spend and its ceilings.

## How to reach it

MCP tools: `command_help`, `job_start`. Tools that take `domain=provider`: `command_help`.

CLI: `stuarty provider` lists the commands of the domain and `stuarty provider <verb>` runs one; the full names stay valid.

- `cost:show`: show the cost ceilings and what was spent today and over the last 30 days
- `provider:list`: list the AI providers, the connections of each and the price table
- `provider:models`: list the models a provider offers through a connection, read from the provider now, with their price
- `text:generate`: generate text with the provider of a connection; the job shows its estimate and waits for approval
- `image:generate`: generate images with the provider of a connection; the job shows its estimate, waits for approval and stores the images as assets
- `voice:generate`: generate speech with the provider of a connection; the job shows its estimate, waits for approval and stores the audio as an asset
- `video:generate`: generate a video clip with the provider of a connection; the job shows its estimate, always waits for a person unless a rule covers it, and stores the clip as an asset
- `voice:list`: list the voices of the provider of a connection

## Operations

No edit operations yet. The domain is reached through the tools and commands above; operations that a later epic registers in it appear here, and `command_help domain=provider` lists them.
