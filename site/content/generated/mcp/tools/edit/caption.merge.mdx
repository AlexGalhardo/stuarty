<RefMain>

The caption that starts first keeps its identifier, style and position and takes the words of the other; the other is removed. Captions with keyframes cannot be merged.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `item_ids` | array of `string` | yes | Exactly two distinct captions of one track, in any order. |
