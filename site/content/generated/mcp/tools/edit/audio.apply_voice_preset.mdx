<RefMain>

One action for a clean, even voice: studio (default), broadcast or room_rescue. The chain of an item (item_id) or a track (track_id) becomes the preset: noise reduction, equaliser, de-esser, compressor and limiter, and the export brings the mix to the loudness_target. Change any processor afterwards with audio.processor_set.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `item_id` | `string` | no | A clip or audio item; give this or track_id.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `preset` | `studio`, `broadcast`, `room_rescue` | no | Studio (default), broadcast or room_rescue. |
| `track_id` | `string` | no | A track; give this or item_id.; pattern `^trk_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
