<RefMain>

Writes the keyframes (or, for slide_left and slide_right, the transition) of the preset on the in or out edge, 0.4 seconds long unless duration is given. Keyframes of the same properties inside that edge are replaced; the rest of the animation is kept.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `duration` | `string` | no | A length, at most the item's duration. Default "0.4s". |
| `edge` | `in`, `out` | yes | In or out. |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | A clip, text, caption or shape item.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `preset` | `fade_up`, `fade_down`, `pop`, `zoom_in`, `slide_left`, `slide_right` | yes | The animation. |
