<RefMain>

Finds the marker at a frame and changes its frame (to) and or its label; a label of "" removes the label. With item_id it works on the markers of that item, and at and to fall inside it.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `at` | `string` | yes | The position of the marker to change. |
| `item_id` | `string` | no | The item that holds the marker; at and to fall inside it.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `label` | `string` | no | The new label, up to 60 characters; "" removes it.; max length 60 |
| `to` | `string` | no | The new position. |

## Errors

- [`E_VALIDATION`](/docs/reference/error-codes#e_validation)

- [`E_NOT_FOUND`](/docs/reference/error-codes#e_not_found)
- [`E_INVALID_STATE`](/docs/reference/error-codes#e_invalid_state)
