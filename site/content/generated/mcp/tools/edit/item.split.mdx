The original item keeps its id and becomes the left part; a new item is the right part. Transitions, fades, keyframes and caption words are divided so that the motion is unchanged.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `at` | `string` | yes | Absolute position strictly inside the item. |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `new_id` | `string` | no | The id of the right part.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `new_ids` | array of `string` | no | The ids of the right parts of the linked items that the split cuts, in the order of the ids of those items. |
