<RefMain>

Writes ordinary keyframes (with a Bezier easing where the preset needs a curve) on the first duration frames (edge in) or the last ones (edge out) of a clip, text, caption or shape item; edit them afterwards with keyframe.set and keyframe.set_easing. Keyframes of the same properties inside that window are replaced, the rest are kept. duration is at most the item's duration, default 0.5s (the whole item for push_in and pull_out). direction is for slide; amount is the slide distance in pixels, the scale factor of scale, or the zoom factor of push_in and pull_out. animation.list_presets describes them.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `amount` | `number` | no | Slide: distance in pixels; scale: factor at the far end; push_in and pull_out: zoom factor. Per preset default. |
| `direction` | `left`, `right`, `up`, `down` | no | Slide only: the side it comes from or leaves to. Default left. |
| `duration` | `string` | no | A length, at most the item's duration. Default 0.5s; the whole item for push_in and pull_out. |
| `edge` | `in`, `out` | no | In animates the start of the item, out its end. Default in. |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | A clip, text, caption or shape item.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `preset` | `fade`, `slide`, `scale`, `pop`, `push_in`, `pull_out` | yes | The animation. |
