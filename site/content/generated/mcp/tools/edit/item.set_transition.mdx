<RefMain>

Sets a fade or a slide on the in or out edge, 0.5 seconds long unless duration is given; kind null removes the transition. The two transitions together cannot be longer than the item.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `duration` | `string` | no | Length of the transition, at least 1 frame; default 0.5s. |
| `edge` | `in`, `out` | yes | The edge: in or out. |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | A clip, text, caption or shape item. |
| `kind` | `fade`, `slide_left`, `slide_right`, `slide_up`, `slide_down`, `null` | yes | Fade, slide_left, slide_right, slide_up, slide_down, or null to remove. |
