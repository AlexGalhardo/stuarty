<RefMain>

Sets a fade, a slide, a zoom, a whip, a dip, a wipe, a spin, a shrink or a grow on the in or out edge, 0.5 seconds long unless duration is given; kind null removes the transition. color (#RRGGBB, default black) is for dip; direction (left, right, up or down, default left) is for whip and wipe. The two transitions together cannot be longer than the item. A transition needs something visible beneath it: to crossfade, let the previous clip run under it, on another track below, so that it is still playing during the duration of the in edge (or already playing during the out edge); otherwise lint reports L_TRANSITION_EMPTY. For example, clip A on the lower track ends at 4.5s and clip B on the upper track starts at 4s, so B gets \{"item_id": B, "edge": "in", "kind": "fade", "duration": "0.5s"\}.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | no | Dip only: #RRGGBB, default #000000.; pattern `^#[0-9A-Fa-f]{6}$` |
| `direction` | `left`, `right`, `up`, `down` | no | Whip and wipe only: left (default), right, up or down. |
| `duration` | `string` | no | Length of the transition, at least 1 frame; default 0.5s. |
| `edge` | `in`, `out` | yes | The edge: in or out. |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | A clip, text, caption or shape item.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `kind` | `string`, one of 13 values | yes | Fade, slide_left, slide_right, slide_up, slide_down, zoom, whip, dip, wipe, spin, shrink, grow, or null to remove. |
