<RefMain>

Moves the items after the gap earlier by the length of the gap: those of the track (ripple track, the default) or those of every track (ripple all); ripple all also moves the timeline markers after the gap and removes those inside it.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `at` | `string` | yes | Absolute frame inside the gap. |
| `ripple` | `track`, `all` | no | Track (default) or all: all moves later items of every track. |
| `track_id` | `string` | yes | The track with the gap.; pattern `^trk_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
