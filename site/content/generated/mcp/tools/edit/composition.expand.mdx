<RefSplit>

<RefMain>

The inverse of composition.create_from_selection, for a composition that only places its timeline: no opacity, mask, effect, transform, keyframe, transition, blend or volume of its own, the whole timeline shown, and the same frame size and rate. The nested timeline stays in the project.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | A composition item.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
