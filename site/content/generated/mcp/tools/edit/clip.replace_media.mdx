<RefMain>

Replaces the asset a clip plays, a video by a video or an image by an image, and keeps its position, duration, trim, speed, transform, keyframes and effects: the animation an external engine rendered again takes the place of the one it replaces. Nothing is stretched or trimmed: a video the clip's range does not fit is refused.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `asset_id` | `string` | yes | A video or image asset of the document, of the same kind as the clip's asset.; pattern `^ast_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
| `item_id` | `string` | yes | A clip.; pattern `^itm_[0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz]{10}$` |
