- [Pan](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#pan): Turning the camera left or right without moving it from its place.
- [Peak](/docs/glossary/audio#peak): The highest point a sound reaches.
- [Pickup shot](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#pickup-shot): A shot filmed later to fill a gap or fix a missing piece.
- [Picture lock](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#picture-lock): The moment when the picture edit is final and cannot change anymore.
- [Picture-in-picture](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#picture-in-picture): A small video shown over a bigger one.
- [Pitch](/docs/glossary/audio#pitch): How high or low a sound is.
- [Pixel](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#pixel): The smallest dot of a picture; millions of them make an image.
- [Pixel format](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#pixel-format): How the colour of each pixel is stored, like yuv420p.
- [Platform](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#platform): The app or website where the video will be watched, like YouTube or TikTok.
- [Playhead](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#playhead): The marker on the timeline that shows the moment you are looking at.
- [PNG](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#png): A picture format that keeps full quality and can be see-through.
- [Point of view](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#point-of-view): A shot that shows what a character sees, as if through their eyes.
- [Post-production](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#post-production): All the work after filming: editing, sound, colour and effects.
- [Poster frame](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#poster-frame): The single frame shown before the video starts.
- [Pre-production](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#pre-production): The planning before filming: script, storyboard, locations and schedule.
- [Preview](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#preview): Watching your video inside the editor while you work.
- [Producer](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#producer): The person who organises the money, the people and the schedule.
- [Production](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#production): The time when the footage is filmed.
- [Project](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#project): The saved file that remembers everything about one video you are making.
- [Project bundle](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#project-bundle): One file that holds a project with all it needs, to move or share it.
- [ProRes](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#prores): A high-quality codec for editing, with big files.
- [Proxy](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#proxy): A small, light copy of a video used to edit smoothly.
- [Punch in](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#punch-in): Zooming into the picture a bit, to make a shot feel closer.

## R

- [Rack focus](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#rack-focus): Changing the focus during a shot, so attention moves from one thing to another.
- [Raw footage](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#raw-footage): Footage straight from the camera, with nothing cut or changed yet.
- [Re-encoding](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#re-encoding): Compressing a video again, which can lower its quality a bit.
- [Reading speed](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#reading-speed): How fast a viewer can read the captions, in characters or words per second.
- [Rec. 2020](/docs/glossary/color#rec-2020): A wider colour standard used for 4K and HDR video.
- [Rec. 709](/docs/glossary/color#rec-709): The standard colour and brightness rules for normal HD video.
- [Render](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#render): Making the computer build the final pictures of the video.
- [Resolution](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#resolution): How many tiny dots (pixels) make up the picture, wide by tall.
- [Reverb](/docs/glossary/audio#reverb): The echo-like tail of a sound in a big space, like a church.
- [Reverse](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#reverse): Playing a clip backwards.
- [Review](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#review): Showing a draft to others and collecting their opinions.
- [Revision](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#revision): A number that goes up by one every time the project is changed.
- [RGB](/docs/glossary/color#rgb): Making every colour by mixing red, green and blue light.
- [Rhythm](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#rhythm): The beat of the cuts, like the beat of a song.
- [Ripple](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#ripple): When one change pushes or pulls everything after it, so no gap is left.
- [Roll](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#roll): Moving the cut between two clips without changing the total length.
- [Room tone](/docs/glossary/audio#room-tone): The quiet sound of a place when nobody is talking.
- [Rotoscoping](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#rotoscoping): Tracing around something frame by frame to cut it out.
- [Rough cut](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#rough-cut): A first, untidy version that has the right pieces in the right order.
- [Runner](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#runner): An entry-level crew member who helps wherever needed.

## S

- [Safe area](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#safe-area): The part of the screen where text will not be cut off or covered.
- [Sample rate](/docs/glossary/audio#sample-rate): How many times per second the sound is measured when it is stored.
- [Saturation](/docs/glossary/color#saturation): How strong or pale the colours are.
- [Scene](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#scene): A part of a story in one place and one time, made of several shots.
- [Score](/docs/glossary/audio#score): The music written for a film or video.
- [Screen](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#screen): A blend mode that makes the picture lighter, where black disappears.
- [Script](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#script): The written plan of what is said and what happens.
- [Scrubbing](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#scrubbing): Dragging the playhead back and forth to look through a video quickly.
- [SDR](/docs/glossary/color#sdr): Standard dynamic range: the ordinary range of normal video.
- [Sensor](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#sensor): The chip in the camera that turns light into the picture.
- [Sepia](/docs/glossary/color#sepia): A warm brown tint that makes a picture look old.
- [Sequence](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#sequence): One timeline with a finished or unfinished video on it.
- [Serif and sans-serif](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#serif-and-sans-serif): Serif letters have little feet; sans-serif letters are plain.
- [Short-form video](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#short-form-video): Videos that last up to a minute or so, made for phones.
- [Shot](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#shot): One piece of video that was filmed without stopping the camera.
- [Shot and reverse shot](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#shot-and-reverse-shot): Switching between two people who are talking to each other.
- [Shot list](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#shot-list): A checklist of all the shots you need to film.
- [Showreel](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#showreel): A short video of someone's best work, used like a portfolio.
- [Shutter speed](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#shutter-speed): How long the camera looks at each frame; short is crisp, long is blurry.
- [Sizzle reel](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#sizzle-reel): A fast, exciting video that shows the best bits to sell an idea.
- [Slate](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#slate): A small board filmed at the start of a take with its name and number.
- [Slide](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#slide): Moving a clip along the timeline while its neighbours stretch to fit.
- [Slip](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#slip): Changing which part of a clip you see, without moving the clip.
- [Slot](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#slot): A named empty place in a template waiting for your media or text.
- [Slow motion](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#slow-motion): Video played slower than real life, so movement looks stretched out.
- [Smash cut](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#smash-cut): A sudden cut from something quiet to something loud, or the other way around.
- [Snapping](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#snapping): Clips stick to edges, markers and the playhead when you drag them.
- [Sound effect](/docs/glossary/audio#sound-effect): A short sound added to match something on screen, like a pop or a whoosh.
- [Source and program monitor](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#source-and-program-monitor): Two screens: one shows the original clip, the other the video you are building.
- [Speaker label](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#speaker-label): A name before a line, to show who is speaking.
- [Speed ramp](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#speed-ramp): Changing the speed of a clip smoothly, from slow to fast or back.
- [Spill](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#spill): Green light bouncing from the screen onto the person, giving a green edge.
- [Split](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#split): Cutting one clip into two at the playhead.
- [Split screen](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#split-screen): Showing two or more videos side by side at the same time.
- [sRGB](/docs/glossary/color#srgb): The standard colour space of the web and most screens.
- [SRT](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#srt): A very common caption file: a list of numbered texts with start and end times.
- [Stabilization](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#stabilization): Making a shaky video smooth, by the camera or by software.
- [Stacking order](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#stacking-order): Which layer is in front of which.
- [Stem](/docs/glossary/audio#stem): A group of sounds mixed together, like all the music or all the voices.
- [Stereo](/docs/glossary/audio#stereo): Sound on two channels, left and right, so things can be placed on either side.
- [Storyboard](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#storyboard): A series of drawings that plan the shots before filming.
- [Stream](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#stream): One kind of data inside a file, such as the video or one sound.
- [Streaming](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#streaming): Watching a video while it downloads, without saving the whole file first.
- [Stroke](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#stroke): An outline around letters, so they can be read on any background.
- [Subtitle](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#subtitle): Words on screen that translate or write down what is said.
- [Surround sound](/docs/glossary/audio#surround-sound): Sound from many speakers around you, like 5.1 in a cinema.

## T

- [Take](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#take): One try at filming a shot.
- [Teaser](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#teaser): A very short video that makes people curious about something coming.
- [Template](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#template): A project saved as a pattern, with empty places you can fill.
- [Text alignment](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#text-alignment): Whether text lines up on the left, in the middle or on the right.
- [Text animation](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#text-animation): Making words move in, out or around.
- [That's a wrap](/docs/glossary/industry-slang#thats-a-wrap): What people say when filming is finished.
- [Thumbnail](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#thumbnail): The small picture that represents a video in a list.
- [Tilt](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#tilt): Pointing the camera up or down without moving it from its place.
- [Time-lapse](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#time-lapse): Many pictures taken far apart in time and played fast, so hours become seconds.
- [Timecode](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#timecode): A clock for video, written as hours:minutes:seconds:frames.
- [Timeline](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#timeline): The strip at the bottom of the editor where you arrange clips in time.
- [Tint](/docs/glossary/color#tint): A little green or pink pushed over the whole picture.
- [Title](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#title): Words at the start of a video, or in it, that say what it is about.
- [Track](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#track): One row of the timeline; clips on the same track play one after another.
- [Tracking shot](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#tracking-shot): A shot where the camera itself travels along with the subject.
- [Trailer](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#trailer): A short preview of a longer film or show.
- [Transcript](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#transcript): The whole text of what is said in a video.
- [Transcription](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#transcription): Turning speech into written words, by a person or by a program.
- [Transform](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#transform): Moving, resizing or turning a layer.
- [Transition](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#transition): A visual effect that moves from one shot to the next.
- [Trim](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#trim): Making a clip shorter by moving its start or its end.
- [Tripod](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#tripod): A three-legged stand that holds the camera still.
- [TTML](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#ttml): A caption file format used by broadcasters and streaming services.
- [Tween](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#tween): The in-between pictures computed between two keyframes.

## U

- [Undo and redo](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#undo-and-redo): Undo takes back your last change; redo puts it back.
- [Unicode](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#unicode): The system that gives a number to every letter, sign and emoji of every language.

## V

- [Vectorscope](/docs/glossary/color#vectorscope): A scope that shows which colours there are and how strong they are.
- [Verification](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#verification): Checking that the exported file is really good before saying it is done.
- [Version](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#version): One saved state of a video, like v1, v2 and v3.
- [Vertical video](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#vertical-video): Video filmed or made tall, like a phone held upright.
- [VFX](/docs/glossary/effects-and-compositing#vfx): Visual effects: pictures made or changed after filming.
- [Video editing](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#video-editing): Choosing the best pieces of footage and putting them in order to tell a story.
- [Vignette](/docs/glossary/color#vignette): Darkening the corners of a picture to draw the eye to the middle.
- [Voice-over](/docs/glossary/audio#voice-over): A voice you hear, but the speaker is not shown speaking.
- [Volume](/docs/glossary/audio#volume): How loud a sound is.
- [VP9](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#vp9): A free codec made by Google, used by YouTube.

## W

- [Watermark](/docs/glossary/delivery-and-platforms#watermark): A faint logo or name placed on a video to show who owns it.
- [WAV](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#wav): A file with sound that is not squeezed, so it keeps full quality.
- [Waveform](/docs/glossary/audio#waveform): The picture of a sound: tall where it is loud, flat where it is quiet.
- [Waveform monitor](/docs/glossary/color#waveform-monitor): A scope that shows how bright every part of the picture is.
- [WebM](/docs/glossary/formats-codecs-containers#webm): An open video format made for the web.
- [WebVTT](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#webvtt): The caption file format used by web browsers.
- [Whip pan](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#whip-pan): A very fast camera turn that blurs the picture, often used to join two shots.
- [White balance](/docs/glossary/color#white-balance): Setting what counts as white, so colours don't look too blue or too orange.
- [Wide shot](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#wide-shot): A shot that shows the whole place, with people looking small.
- [Wipe](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#wipe): A line sweeps across the screen and the new shot appears behind it.
- [Word timing](/docs/glossary/text-and-captions#word-timing): The exact start and end of every word, so words appear in time with the voice.

## Z

- [Zoom](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#zoom): Changing the lens so the subject looks closer or farther away.
