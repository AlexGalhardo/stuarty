Terms on this page: 32. Compositing means stacking pictures like sheets of glass. These words explain the stack and the tricks you do with it.

### Alpha channel

Besides red, green and blue, some images keep a fourth value, alpha, for each pixel: 0 is invisible, 1 is solid. A logo with a transparent background has an alpha channel.

**Example.** A PNG logo with no white box around it.

**Also called:** alpha, RGBA. **See also:** [Opacity](#opacity), [Mask](#mask).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Alpha compositing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_compositing)

### Anchor point

A layer rotates around its anchor point like a door around its hinge. Move the anchor and the same turn looks different.

**Example.** A clock hand turns around its centre, its anchor.

**See also:** [Transform](#transform).

### Animation

In editing, animation means changing position, size, turn or see-through-ness over time.

**Example.** A title slides in, stays, and slides out.

**See also:** [Keyframe](#keyframe), [Motion graphics](#motion-graphics).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animation)

### Blend mode

Normal just covers what is below. Other modes add, multiply or lighten the colours, to make glows, shadows and light leaks.

**Example.** A sparkle video on a black background looks good when its blend mode is Screen: the black disappears.

**In Vukem Video.** `clip.set_blend`: normal, multiply, screen, overlay or add. See [Effects and color](/docs/guide/effects-and-color).

**Also called:** transfer mode. **See also:** [Multiply](#multiply), [Screen](#screen), [Overlay (blend mode)](#overlay-blend-mode).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Blend modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blend_modes)

### Blur

Blur hides detail. Editors use it to hide faces or car plates, or to make a background soft.

**Example.** A blurred copy of the video fills the empty bars of a vertical video.

**In Vukem Video.** `clip.set_blur`, a radius from 0 to 100 pixels.

**Also called:** Gaussian blur. **See also:** [Filter](#filter), [Motion blur](#motion-blur).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Gaussian blur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_blur)

### CGI

CGI means 3D models and animation created in software, then placed into a video.

**Example.** A talking animal in a family film.

**Also called:** computer-generated imagery. **See also:** [VFX](#vfx).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Computer-generated imagery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery)

### Chroma key

You film a person in front of a plain green wall. The editor makes every green pixel see-through, so you can place any background behind.

**Example.** A kid sits in front of green cloth and the video puts them on the Moon.

**In Vukem Video.** `clip.set_chroma_key` sets the colour, the similarity, the smoothness and the spill. See [Effects and color](/docs/guide/effects-and-color).

**Also called:** green screen, blue screen, keying. **See also:** [Spill](#spill), [Matte](#matte).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Chroma key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key) · [FFmpeg: filters documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html)

### Compositing

Compositing stacks layers. A person filmed in front of a green screen, a drawing of a forest and a title can become one scene.

**Example.** A weather reporter in front of a moving map.

**In Vukem Video.** Stacked tracks are composited, and the preview and the export use the same render plan. See [Render](/docs/concepts/render).

**See also:** [Layer](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#layer), [Chroma key](#chroma-key), [Blend mode](#blend-mode).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Compositing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compositing)

### Crop

Cropping removes the sides, the top or the bottom, to hide something or change the shape.

**Example.** Crop a wide video so a person fills a tall frame.

**In Vukem Video.** `clip.set_framing` has a crop for each side. See [Clip properties](/docs/guide/clip-properties).

**See also:** [Fit](#fit), [Aspect ratio](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#aspect-ratio).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Cropping (image)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cropping_(image))

### Effect

Blur, glow, a colour change, an echo: effects change a clip without changing the original file.

**Example.** A zoom pulse on every beat.

**See also:** [Filter](#filter), [VFX](#vfx).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Visual effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_effects)

### Feather

A feathered edge fades slowly from visible to hidden, so the shape blends in smoothly.

**Example.** A round spotlight with a soft border.

**In Vukem Video.** `feather`, in pixels from 0 to 200, in `clip.set_mask`.

**See also:** [Mask](#mask).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Feathering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feathering)

### Filter

A filter changes colours or details in one step, like making it black and white or warm.

**Example.** A sepia filter on an old family video.

**In Vukem Video.** `clip.set_filter`: grayscale, sepia, invert, warm or cool, with an amount.

**See also:** [Look](/docs/glossary/color#look), [Color grading](/docs/glossary/color#color-grading).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Filter (signal processing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filter_(signal_processing)) · [FFmpeg: filters documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html)

### Fit

Cover fills the whole frame and cuts what does not fit. Contain shows all of the picture and leaves empty bars. None keeps the original size.

**Example.** A wide video in a tall frame: cover cuts the sides; contain shows black bars above and below.

**In Vukem Video.** `fit` is `cover`, `contain` or `none` in `clip.set_framing`.

**Also called:** cover, contain, letterbox. **See also:** [Aspect ratio](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#aspect-ratio), [Crop](#crop).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Letterboxing (filming)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letterboxing_(filming))

### Keyframe

Instead of drawing every frame, you say: at second 1 the box is here, at second 3 it is there. The computer works out the movement between.

**Example.** A star that starts small at 0 seconds and ends big at 2 seconds.

**In Vukem Video.** `keyframe.set` for x, y, scale, rotation, opacity and volume. See [Animate with keyframes](/docs/guide/keyframes).

**See also:** [Easing](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#easing), [Tween](#tween), [Animation](#animation).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Key frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_frame)

### Mask

A mask hides everything outside its shape. You can use it to show a video through a circle, or to hide an unwanted thing.

**Example.** Showing a face inside a round bubble.

**In Vukem Video.** `clip.set_mask` limits an item to a rectangle or an ellipse, with a soft edge and an invert option.

**See also:** [Feather](#feather), [Matte](#matte).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Mask (computing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mask_(computing)) · [Wikipedia: Matte (filmmaking)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matte_(filmmaking))

### Matte

A matte is black where you hide and white where you show. It is a mask saved as an image, or a clip.

**Example.** A black and white drawing of a bird used to cut a bird shape out of a video.

**Also called:** track matte. **See also:** [Mask](#mask), [Alpha channel](#alpha-channel).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Matte (filmmaking)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matte_(filmmaking))

### Motion blur

When an object moves during the time the camera takes a frame, it leaves a streak. It makes movement look smooth and real.

**Example.** A spinning fan blade looks like a soft circle.

**See also:** [Shutter speed](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#shutter-speed), [Frame rate](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#frame-rate).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Motion blur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_blur)

### Motion graphics

Motion graphics are moving designs: titles that fly, charts that grow, shapes that bounce.

**Example.** An animated bar chart in a news video.

**In Vukem Video.** `shape.add` creates rectangles, rounded rectangles and ellipses to animate. See [Shapes](/docs/guide/shapes).

**See also:** [Animation](#animation), [Keyframe](#keyframe).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Motion graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_graphics)

### Motion tracking

A tracker finds a spot in every frame. You can then attach text or a blur that stays on that spot as it moves.

**Example.** A name tag that follows a player across the field.

**See also:** [Stabilization](/docs/glossary/shooting-and-footage#stabilization), [Blur](#blur).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Match moving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Match_moving)

### Multiply

Multiply mixes the colours of the layer with those below and always gives a darker result. White does nothing.

**Example.** A paper texture on top of a video makes it look printed.

**See also:** [Blend mode](#blend-mode), [Screen](#screen).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Blend modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blend_modes)

### Opacity

Lower the opacity and the layer becomes see-through, so what is under it shows.

**Example.** A watermark set to 30% so it does not hide the video.

**In Vukem Video.** `opacity` is a property of an item and can be keyframed, from 0 to 1.

**Also called:** transparency. **See also:** [Alpha channel](#alpha-channel), [Fade](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#fade).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Opacity (optics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opacity_(optics))

### Overlay

In editing, an overlay is an extra layer drawn over the main video.

**Example.** A channel logo in the corner of the screen.

**See also:** [Layer](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#layer), [Picture-in-picture](#picture-in-picture).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Digital on-screen graphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_on-screen_graphic)

### Overlay (blend mode)

Overlay combines multiply and screen, so it increases contrast and keeps the lightest and the darkest points.

**Example.** A texture that gives a picture more punch.

**See also:** [Blend mode](#blend-mode).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Blend modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blend_modes)

### Picture-in-picture

Picture-in-picture puts a small window on top of the main video. It is used for reactions and tutorials.

**Example.** A gamer's face in the corner of the game footage.

**Also called:** PiP. **See also:** [Overlay](#overlay), [Transform](#transform).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Picture-in-picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picture-in-picture)

### Rotoscoping

Rotoscoping draws the outline of a person or an object on every frame. It is slow work, used when no green screen exists.

**Example.** Cutting a person out of a normal video so the background can change.

**See also:** [Mask](#mask), [Matte](#matte).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Rotoscoping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotoscoping)

### Screen

Screen is the opposite of multiply and always makes things lighter. Black does nothing.

**Example.** Fire or sparkles filmed on black are placed on a scene with Screen.

**See also:** [Blend mode](#blend-mode), [Multiply](#multiply).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Blend modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blend_modes)

### Spill

Light from a green screen reflects on hair and shoulders. After keying, a green fringe remains, and spill removal takes the green out of it.

**Example.** A green glow around someone's hair.

**In Vukem Video.** `spill`, from 0 to 1, in `clip.set_chroma_key`.

**Also called:** despill. **See also:** [Chroma key](#chroma-key).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Chroma key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key)

### Split screen

The frame is divided into parts and each shows a different video.

**Example.** Two friends on a phone call, shown side by side.

**See also:** [Picture-in-picture](#picture-in-picture), [Crop](#crop).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Split screen (filmmaking)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_screen_(filmmaking))

### Stacking order

Layers are drawn from bottom to top. The one on top covers the others unless it is see-through.

**Example.** The caption is placed above the video, so you see it.

**In Vukem Video.** The preview and the export draw the tracks from the bottom row up, so the track at the top of the timeline is drawn on top.

**Also called:** z-order. **See also:** [Layer](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#layer), [Track](/docs/glossary/editing-and-timeline#track).

### Transform

Transform changes where a layer is (position), how big (scale) and how rotated.

**Example.** Make a logo smaller and put it in the corner.

**In Vukem Video.** `item.set_transform` sets x, y, scale, rotation and opacity.

**See also:** [Anchor point](#anchor-point), [Crop](#crop).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Transformation (function)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_(function))

### Tween

"Tween" is short for in-between. Artists draw the key poses and tweening fills the rest.

**Example.** A ball keyframed at the top and at the bottom: the falling is the tween.

**Also called:** inbetweening. **See also:** [Keyframe](#keyframe), [Easing](/docs/glossary/cuts-and-transitions#easing).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Inbetweening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbetweening)

### VFX

VFX is the work that adds what the camera could not film: explosions, creatures, removed wires.

**Example.** A dragon flying over a real village.

**Also called:** visual effects. **See also:** [Compositing](#compositing), [CGI](#cgi).

**Learn more:** [Wikipedia: Visual effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_effects)
