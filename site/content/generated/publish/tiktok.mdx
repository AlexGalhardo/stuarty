Stuarty publishes to TikTok through a developer app that you create and own (ADR-0035): no Stuarty account stands between you and TikTok, and the secret stays in your vault.

## Permissions and hosts

The sign-in asks for these permissions and no others:

- `user.info.basic`
- `video.upload`

The only hosts Stuarty contacts for TikTok: `open.tiktokapis.com`, `open-upload.tiktokapis.com`. They are contacted only after you create the connection.

The official guide is [here](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-get-started-upload-content) (read on 2026-11-20).

## Limits

These are the limits Stuarty checks a post to TikTok against before anything is sent (`publish:validate`). A post that breaks one is refused with the limit named, and nothing leaves your machine.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title | at most 100 characters |
| Video file | at most 4 GB |
| Video formats | mp4 |
| Video codecs | h264, hevc |
| Longest video | 10 min |
| Frame rate | 23 to 60 fps |
| Smallest picture | 360 x 360 pixels |
| Widest picture | 4096 pixels |
| Export size of the preset | 1080 x 1920 |
| Thumbnail | not sent |
| Visibility | private |
| Posts through the API | 5 in a moving 24 hours |

### Where the numbers come from

- [TikTok: the page for the post](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide), read on 2026-11-20.
- [The page for the video](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide), read on 2026-11-19: MP4, WebM or MOV; H.264 recommended and H.265 supported; 23 to 60 fps; 360 to 4096 pixels per side; 4 GB; up to 10 minutes through the upload endpoint.

TikTok takes the file as a draft in your own app: it is never public until you finish it there.
