Stuarty publishes to YouTube through a developer app that you create and own (ADR-0035): no Stuarty account stands between you and YouTube, and the secret stays in your vault.

## Permissions and hosts

The sign-in asks for these permissions and no others:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly`

The only hosts Stuarty contacts for YouTube: `www.googleapis.com`, `oauth2.googleapis.com`. They are contacted only after you create the connection.

The official guide is [here](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/guides/auth/server-side-web-apps) (read on 2026-11-19).

## Limits

These are the limits Stuarty checks a post to YouTube against before anything is sent (`publish:validate`). A post that breaks one is refused with the limit named, and nothing leaves your machine.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title | at most 100 characters |
| Description | at most 5000 bytes |
| Tags | at most 500 characters in all |
| Video file | at most 274.9 GB |
| Video formats | mp4 |
| Thumbnail | at most 52.4 MB, png, jpg, jpeg |
| Visibility | private, unlisted, public |

### Where the numbers come from

- [YouTube: the page for the post](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos), read on 2026-11-19.

YouTube publishes only after you approve the post.
