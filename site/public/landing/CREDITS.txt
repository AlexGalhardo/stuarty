Screenshots of Stuarty v0.52.0, captured on 2026-11-24 with a headless Chromium from a local `stuarty serve` of the
repository (release branch v0.53.0), on a throwaway demo workspace made for the capture (epic 88 task 3).

Every image is a capture of the released user interface at 1600 x 1000 px, in the dark theme (name.*) and in the light
theme (name-light.*), as .webp and .avif at 1600 px and, with the suffix -960, at 960 px. The 960 px files are the
1600 px capture scaled down. Nothing is drawn or composed: no design file, render or mock-up.

Media shown inside the screenshots: none from outside the repository. The clips, pictures and sounds of the demo
workspace were generated for the capture (gradients and sine tones made with the project's own FFmpeg). The demo
content (a text project, a video project, an image project, a project not decided yet, and a few skills, prompts and
memory notes about an invented community garden) is invented: it names no real person, brand or number, and it holds
no logo, testimonial or metric. The agent profiles and the home folder shown are the defaults of the machine that
captured them; no account, key or token of any user appears.
