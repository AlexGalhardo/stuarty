# Names of folders, files and ids

1. Product code keeps its layout (ADR-0001): `core/`, `engine/`, `renderer/`, `service/`, `web/`, `site/`, `desktop/`, `internal/`, `scripts/`, `docs/`. Do not rename product folders or files to follow a convention: imports, CI and history depend on them.
2. Files and folders are kebab-case, lowercase, ASCII, except the canonical files at the top of a folder (`README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `LICENSE.md`, `SKILL.md`, and in `development/` also `TODO.md`, `PROMPT.md`, `QUESTIONS.md`, `PLAN.md`, `LICENSES.md`). Go and TypeScript files follow their language conventions.
3. `docs/` keeps its numbered documents: `prds/NNN-<slug>.md` (requirements `RF-NNN`, `NFR-NNN`), `adrs/NNNN-<slug>.md` (`ADR-NNNN`), `sdds/NNN-<slug>.md` (`SDD-NNN`, section references `SDD-NNN §n`), Decisions `D-NNN` and research notes are private (D-037): `development/brainstorming.md` and `development/search/<slug>.md`; public documents cite them as "author decision D-NNN" and "research note `<slug>`", never as a link or a path. A new document takes the next free number; an existing file is never renumbered or moved, because code and other documents cite it.
4. Every document in `docs/` starts with a level-one title and a short paragraph that says what it answers; PRDs, ADRs and SDDs keep their status line and their numbered sections.
5. Ids are unique, global and never reused: `RF-`, `NFR-`, `ADR-`, `SDD-`, `D-` (docs); `EPIC-`, `TASK-`, `VV-`, `VF-`, `TV-`, `B-`, rounds `R<n>` (`development/`, rule `development-folder.md`); error codes `E_*` and lint rules `L_*` (SDD-000).
6. Branches, commits and releases follow `rules/git-and-branches.md` and `rules/commits.md`.
