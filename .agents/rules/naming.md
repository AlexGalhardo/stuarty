# Names of folders, files and ids

1. Product code keeps its layout (ADR-0001): `core/`, `engine/`, `renderer/`, `service/`, `web/`, `site/`, `desktop/`, `internal/`, `scripts/`, `docs/`. Do not rename product folders or files to follow a convention: imports, CI and history depend on them. The one planned exception is the rename of the product to Stuarty, done once by its own epic (ADR-0044); until it is merged, write "Stuarty" in prose and the identifier the code accepts (`vukem-video`, `VUKEM_VIDEO_*`) in commands.
2. Files and folders are kebab-case, lowercase, ASCII, except the canonical files at the top of a folder (`README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `LICENSE.md`, `REVIEW.md`, `SKILL.md`, and in `development/` also `PROMPT.md`, `QUESTIONS.md`, `PLAN.md`, `LICENSES.md`). Go, TypeScript and Rust files follow their language conventions.
3. `docs/` keeps its numbered documents: `prds/NNN-<slug>.md` (requirements `RF-NNN`, `NFR-NNN`), `adrs/NNNN-<slug>.md` (`ADR-NNNN`), `sdds/NNN-<slug>.md` (`SDD-NNN`, section references `SDD-NNN §n`), and `plans/NN-<slug>.md` (epic NN, the units of work, rule `plans.md`) with the index `plans/00-roadmap.md`. Decisions `D-NNN` and research notes are private: public documents cite them as "author decision D-NNN" and "research note `<slug>`", never as a link or a path. A new document takes the next number; a document is not renumbered, and it is renamed only when its name states something that is no longer true.
4. Every document in `docs/` starts with a level-one title and a short paragraph that says what it answers; PRDs, ADRs and SDDs keep their status line and their numbered sections. An ADR is not edited to reverse it: a new ADR supersedes or amends it, and the status line of the old one says so.
5. Ids are unique, global and never reused: `RF-`, `NFR-`, `ADR-`, `SDD-`, `D-` (documents); `VV-`, `VF-`, `TV-`, `B-`, rounds `R<n>` and `L<n>` (`development/`, rule `development-folder.md`); error codes `E_*` and lint rules `L_*` (SDD-000). Epics are numbered by their file, `NN`, and their tasks by position.
6. The team's agents are named by role in `.claude/agents/`: `senior-a` to `senior-e`, `intern`, `specialist`.
7. Branches and commits follow `rules/git-and-branches.md` and `rules/commits.md`: `epic/NN-<slug>` and `fix/VV-NNN-<slug>`, from the integration branch.
